About Us

Small studio, big ideas.

FritzTudios is an indie game studio dedicated to crafting tight, polished, and memorable gaming experiences. We love clean design, satisfying mechanics, and games that respect your time.

🎮

Player First

Every design decision starts with one question: is this fun? We prototype fast, cut ruthlessly, and polish until it feels just right.

✦

Clean & Minimal

We believe the best games have a strong visual identity and mechanics you can learn in seconds but enjoy for hours.

📱

Cross-Platform

Our games are designed to feel native on every device — from mobile to desktop — with controls optimized for each platform.

🔁

Always Improving

We listen closely to player feedback and ship regular updates to keep our games fresh and our community happy.
